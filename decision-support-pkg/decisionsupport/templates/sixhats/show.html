How it works
  1. Setup: Describe the topic or decision to explore.
  2. Open a hat: Open the first hat and share the participant link. Participants contribute thoughts from that hat’s perspective.
  3. Progress: Close each hat when the group is done and open the next. Each hat brings a distinct thinking mode — follow the order for best results.
  4. Complete: Once all six hats are closed the session completes automatically. Review the full picture of contributions before making a decision.
